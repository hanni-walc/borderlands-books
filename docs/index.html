About Us

Borderlands Books resides in San Francisco’s historic Haight-Ashbury neighborhood. We specialize in science fiction, fantasy, mystery, and horror titles, along with a curated selection of literary fiction and graphic novels.

We host regular author readings, book clubs, and gaming nights—creating a vibrant community hub for readers of all ages.
